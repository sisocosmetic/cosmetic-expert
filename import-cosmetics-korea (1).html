Guide for Foreign Cosmetic Brands

How to Import Cosmetics
into Korea

Korea does not require product approval for most cosmetics, but every product must be placed on the market by a licensed Korean company and meet Korean ingredient, labeling and advertising rules. Here is what happens between your decision to enter Korea and your first sale, step by step.

The process at a glance

  1. Appoint a Korean Responsible Distributor
  2. Check the formula against Korean ingredient rules
  3. Prepare the import documents
  4. Import, customs clearance and quality testing
  5. Sell, and keep up post-market duties

Step by step

From first contact to first sale

01

Appoint a Korean Responsible Distributor

Only a company registered with Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) as a Cosmetic Responsible Distributor (화장품책임판매업자) can import cosmetics and place them on the Korean market. This company carries the legal responsibility for the products in Korea, and its name appears on every Korean label.

You can set up your own Korean entity and register it, or appoint an independent Responsible Distributor. Most brands start with an independent one, because it lets them enter Korea without a local office or staff.

What SISOCOSMETIC doesWe act as your Responsible Distributor, so you can enter Korea without opening a Korean company.
02

Check the formula against Korean ingredient rules

Korea keeps its own lists of prohibited ingredients and of restricted ingredients with maximum concentrations, for example preservatives, UV filters and colorants. A product that is compliant in the EU or the US is not automatically compliant in Korea.

  • Full formula with percentages, or your EU Product Information File (PIF)
  • Check of every ingredient against Korean prohibited and restricted lists
  • Decide whether the product is a general or a functional cosmetic

Functional cosmetics, such as whitening (brightening), anti-wrinkle, sunscreen, hair-loss relief or acne-care products, need an MFDS review or report before they can be sold with those claims.

What SISOCOSMETIC doesWe review your formula or PIF before anything ships, and tell you early if an ingredient, concentration or claim needs to change for Korea.
03

Prepare the import documents

The exact set depends on the product and the country of manufacture. A typical first import uses:

  • Certificate of Free Sale or manufacturer's certificate
  • BSE/TSE-free statement where relevant (animal-derived ingredients)
  • Full ingredient list with INCI names
  • Product specifications and certificate of analysis
What SISOCOSMETIC doesWe send you a document checklist for your products and check each document before shipment, so nothing is held at customs.
04

Import, customs clearance and quality testing

Before customs clearance, the Responsible Distributor files a standard customs pre-clearance report through the Korea Cosmetic Association, and reports the ingredients used before the products are distributed. Imported cosmetics also need quality testing by batch in Korea before release, unless an exemption applies.

What SISOCOSMETIC doesWe handle the reports, customs coordination and batch release on your behalf.
05

Sell, and keep up post-market duties

Once released, products can be sold through Korean retailers and online platforms. The Responsible Distributor continues to handle safety information and adverse event reporting, annual reporting and record keeping.

FAQ

Common questions

Can a foreign brand import cosmetics into Korea without a local company?

No. Cosmetics must be placed on the Korean market by a company registered with MFDS as a Cosmetic Responsible Distributor. You can appoint an independent one, such as SISOCOSMETIC, instead of setting up your own Korean entity.

Do I need to register each product with MFDS before selling?

General cosmetics do not need product approval, but they must comply with Korean ingredient, labeling and advertising rules. Functional cosmetics, such as whitening, anti-wrinkle or sunscreen products, need an MFDS review or report first.

Can I use my existing English or EU label?

You need Korean labeling, but you do not need new packaging. A Korean sticker on your existing packaging is common, as long as it carries all required information.

Do you work with resellers or parallel imports?

No. We work directly with brand owners or with companies authorized by the brand.

Can my products be sold online in Korea before I have a distributor?

Yes. We can import under our license and run online sales on Naver, Coupang, Musinsa and W Concept on your behalf.
