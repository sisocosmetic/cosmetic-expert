The Challenge

Why Most Brands
Struggle in Korea

⚔️

Intensely Competitive Market

Korean consumers are among the most informed and demanding in the world. Domestic brands dominate shelves, and foreign brands must earn their place with a sharp, localized positioning strategy.

⚖️

Pricing & Regulatory Complexity

Korea's cosmetic regulations are strict and frequently updated. Mispriced or non-compliant products get pulled from shelves — or never make it on. Getting this right from the start is non-negotiable.

📉

High Rate of Failed Launches

Without the right channel, timing, and messaging, even high-quality products disappear within months. A beautiful product alone is not enough — you need a Korea-specific strategy.

What We Do

Four Pillars of
Market Entry

01

Market Assessment

We evaluate your brand's fit for the Korean market — category sizing, competitor landscape, consumer trends, and realistic opportunity mapping. You'll know exactly where you stand before committing resources.

02

Pricing Strategy

Pricing in Korea is nuanced — import duties, retailer margins, consumer price sensitivity, and brand positioning all interact. We model the right price architecture for your brand to be competitive and profitable.

03

Channel Strategy

Olive Young, department stores, H&B, e-commerce, social commerce — each channel has different dynamics. We identify the right starting point for your brand and map the path to expand from there.

04

Regulatory Overview & Launch Roadmap

A clear picture of compliance requirements, timelines, and key milestones — so your launch is not just planned, but executable. We connect this directly to our import and RP services when you're ready to move.
