Korea Cosmetics Regulations

The Korean Responsible Person
for Cosmetics

Every cosmetic sold in Korea must have a registered Korean company — the Responsible Distributor — that takes legal responsibility for it. For foreign brands, understanding this role is the starting point for entering Korea.

Key points

  • Every cosmetic sold in Korea must have a Korean Responsible Distributor (화장품책임판매업자)
  • Only a Korean-incorporated company can hold this registration with MFDS
  • Foreign brands can appoint an independent Responsible Distributor without opening a Korean office
  • The Responsible Distributor's name and address must appear on every product label
  • The Responsible Distributor is legally accountable for safety, labeling, and advertising compliance

What it is

The legal foundation of selling cosmetics in Korea

What a Responsible Distributor is

Under Korea's Cosmetics Act (화장품법), a Cosmetic Responsible Distributor (화장품책임판매업자) is a company registered with the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS, 식품의약품안전처) that is legally responsible for cosmetics placed on the Korean market.

This is a mandatory requirement. No cosmetic — whether manufactured in Korea or imported — can be legally sold in Korea without a registered Responsible Distributor. The registration is an ongoing status, not a one-time approval of a specific product.

In practiceThe Responsible Distributor is the company MFDS contacts for any question about a product, the company whose name is on the label, and the company that handles post-market duties. If something goes wrong, they are the first to answer.

Who can be a Responsible Distributor

Only a company legally incorporated in Korea can register as a Cosmetic Responsible Distributor. The registration is held at a specific business address in Korea. A foreign company, however large, cannot hold the registration directly.

For foreign brands, there are two routes:

  • Set up a Korean subsidiary or branch and register it with MFDS — this gives the brand full control but requires a Korean entity, local staff, and ongoing compliance management
  • Appoint an independent Korean Responsible Distributor — this lets the brand enter Korea without a Korean entity; the independent distributor holds the registration and handles regulatory duties under a commercial agreement with the brand

Most brands entering Korea for the first time choose an independent Responsible Distributor. It is faster, requires less capital, and allows the brand to test the market before committing to a Korean entity.

Responsibilities

What the Responsible Distributor is accountable for

Quality control and product safety

Before any product is released to the Korean market, the Responsible Distributor must verify that it meets Korean quality standards. This includes checking that the formula complies with Korean ingredient rules, that the product has been manufactured according to good manufacturing practice, and that quality testing has been carried out.

For imported products, batch quality testing by a Korean laboratory or under a recognized testing scheme is required before products are released for sale. The Responsible Distributor keeps records of every batch.

Ingredient compliance and product information file

The Responsible Distributor must ensure every ingredient in every product is permitted under Korean rules — including prohibited substances, restricted substances with maximum concentrations, and special rules for preservatives, UV filters, and colorants. Korean rules differ from EU, US, and Japanese rules, so compliance in another market does not automatically mean compliance in Korea.

For general cosmetics, a product information file (제품별 안전성 자료) must be kept by the Responsible Distributor — not submitted to MFDS in advance, but held for inspection. For functional cosmetics, the specific product must be reviewed or reported to MFDS before it can be sold with the relevant functional claim.

Labeling and advertising oversight

The Responsible Distributor is responsible for ensuring Korean labels are accurate and compliant, and that advertising and marketing materials — including online store listings and social media — do not make prohibited claims. The Responsible Distributor's name and address must appear on the label; this cannot be replaced by the foreign brand's details.

Post-market duties

After products are on sale, the Responsible Distributor's duties continue:

  • Collecting and reporting adverse events to MFDS within required timeframes
  • Maintaining safety monitoring records
  • Submitting annual reports to MFDS on products distributed
  • Cooperating with any MFDS inspection or recall action
  • Managing product withdrawals or recalls if safety issues arise

FAQ

Common questions

Can a foreign brand import cosmetics into Korea without a local company?

No. Every cosmetic sold in Korea must have a Korean Responsible Distributor registered with MFDS. A foreign brand must either set up a Korean entity and register it, or appoint an independent Korean Responsible Distributor.

Does using a Responsible Distributor mean I lose control of my brand in Korea?

No. The Responsible Distributor handles regulatory duties, but the brand retains ownership of its products and intellectual property. The commercial agreement between the brand and the distributor sets out how decisions are made, what the distributor can and cannot do, and what happens if either party ends the arrangement.

What happens if I want to change my Responsible Distributor?

You can appoint a new Responsible Distributor. The transition requires the new distributor to take over records and duties, and products still on sale with the old distributor's label must work through before or be relabeled. We recommend planning this carefully to avoid a gap in market compliance.

Can my Responsible Distributor also sell my products?

Yes. A Responsible Distributor can also act as a sales agent or distributor for the brand, depending on the commercial agreement. Some brands keep these roles separate; others combine them. SISOCOSMETIC can act as both Responsible Distributor and online sales operator for your brand in Korea.

Is the Responsible Distributor the same as a cosmetic manufacturer registration?

No. Cosmetic manufacturers (화장품제조업자) need a separate registration from MFDS. The Responsible Distributor registration covers companies that place finished cosmetics on the Korean market. If you use a Korean contract manufacturer, they hold the manufacturer registration; you or your appointed Responsible Distributor holds the distributor registration.
